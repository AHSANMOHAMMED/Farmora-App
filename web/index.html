Direct Farm-Gate Produce with Zero Middlemen

Farmora revolutionizes Sri Lanka's agricultural supply chain by eliminating exploitative middlemen. Farmers post their fresh harvest with photos, grading certificates, and transparent pricing. Wholesalers and consumers buy directly from the farm.

  • Nuwara Eliya & Up-Country Produce: Fresh carrots, leeks, potatoes, cabbage, beetroot, and bell peppers.
  • Low-Country Produce: Tomatoes, brinjals, okra, snake gourd, bitter gourd, and pumpkin.
  • Authentic Ceylon Spices: Organic cinnamon, black pepper, cloves, and cardamom directly from Matale and Kandy.
  • Grains & Staples: Red raw rice, samba, keeri samba, and organic pulses.

Integrated Cold-Chain Transport & Real-Time Logistics

Prevent post-harvest food waste through Farmora's integrated logistics network. Book verified transporters with refrigerated trucks, lorries, and three-wheelers across major hubs including Dambulla, Nuwara Eliya, Colombo, Kandy, and Jaffna. Features digital proof-of-delivery (POD) and real-time trip GPS tracking.
