The Leading Digital Farming Market in Sri Lanka

Farmora (FARMORA) transforms traditional farming into a transparent digital farming market. Farmers sell directly at farm-gate prices, avoiding predatory middlemen. Wholesalers and supermarkets purchase verified fresh produce with complete quality trace certificates.

  • Farming Crops & Vegetables: Carrots, leeks, potatoes, cabbage, beetroot, tomatoes, brinjals, okra, snake gourd, and pumpkin.
  • Fruits: Papaya, banana, mango, pineapple, avocado, and watermelon.
  • Pure Ceylon Spices: Organic cinnamon, black pepper, cloves, and cardamom.
  • Grains: Red raw rice, white raw rice, samba, keeri samba, and pulses.

Agricultural Logistics, Farm-to-Market Delivery & Fleet Booking

Prevent harvest food waste through FARMORA's integrated agricultural logistics network. Book verified transporters with refrigerated trucks, lorries, and pickup vehicles across major farming hubs. Features real-time GPS tracking and digital proof-of-delivery (POD).

Trilingual Farming Application (ගොවි ඇප් / விவசாய செயலி)

Farmora is fully localized in Sinhala (සිංහල), Tamil (தமிழ்), and English. Voice notes, offline mode, and SMS order alerts make it accessible to every rural farming community across Sri Lanka.
