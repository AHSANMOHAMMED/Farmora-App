### 1.2 Positioning Statement
> *"For commercial agricultural producers, wholesale buyers, and logistics operators in Sri Lanka who are burdened by predatory middlemen and cold-chain losses, **Farmora** is an end-to-end digital ecosystem that provides transparent price discovery, video-verified crop quality, and escrow-protected logistics. Unlike traditional physical economic centres and fragmented broker cartels, Farmora guarantees fair income, zero default risk, and verified food safety directly from farm to retail."*

## 2. Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

### Phase 1: Pilot Launch & Grassroots Activation (Months 1–3)
- **Target Hub:** Dambulla and Nuwara Eliya agricultural belts.
- **On-the-ground Farmer Ambassadors:** Partner with local Agrarian Service Centres and tea/vegetable farmer co-operatives to onboard the first 250 farmers.
- **Incentive:** 0% commission on the first 5 transactions + free batch QR sticker printer access at local depots.

### Phase 2: Wholesale Buyer & Supermarket Integration (Months 4–6)
- **Target Buyers:** Supermarket chains (Keells, Cargills, Arpico), major hotels, and Colombo wholesale catering syndicates.
- **Value Delivery:** Dedicated procurement dashboard with bulk invoicing and automated cold-chain audit certificates.

### Phase 3: Regional Expansion & Logistics Network (Months 7–12)
- Expansion into Jaffna, Anuradhapura, and Matale districts.
- Full commercial rollout of the Transporter Fleet Hub for cross-island cold chain transport.

## 3. Business & Monetization Model

Farmora operates on a sustainable multi-stream revenue model:

| Revenue Stream | Model Type | Pricing Structure | Target Segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marketplace Transaction Fee** | Commission | 2.5% per completed wholesale order (deducted from buyer payment) | Wholesale Buyers |
| **Escrow & Quality Guarantee** | Escrow Assurance | 1.0% escrow protection fee ensuring payment custody & refund dispute cover | All Marketplace Users |
| **Logistics Freight Matching** | Platform Fee | 3.5% per dispatch trip matched to independent fleet operators | Transporters |
| **Enterprise SaaS Dashboard** | Subscription | $25/month for multi-branch warehouse analytics & ERP inventory API sync | Supermarkets & Exporters |
| **Agri-Fintech Micro-loans** | Partner Revenue Share | 1.2% origination fee on micro-credit facilities underwritten with bank partners | Farmers |

## 4. Emerging UX Trends Tie-In
- **AI-Powered Harvest Visual Inspection:** Computer vision analyzes uploaded harvest photos to recommend initial quality grading (Grade A/B/C) and alert farmers if leaf discoloration suggests blight.
- **Voice UX & Conversational Local Dialects:** Integrated natural-language voice input enabling farmers in rural fields to search market prices and list products hands-free in colloquial Sinhala and Tamil.
- **Augmented Reality (AR) Produce Sizing:** Prototype AR camera overlay allowing buyers to inspect average fruit and vegetable dimensions before placing bulk orders.
