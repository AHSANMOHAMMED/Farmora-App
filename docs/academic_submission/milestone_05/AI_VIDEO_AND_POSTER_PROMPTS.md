# 🎬 1. InVideo AI / HeyGen (One-Click All-in-One Generator Prompts)

If you are using **InVideo AI** (`ai.invideo.io`) or **HeyGen** (`heygen.com`), paste the complete prompt below into the prompt box. The AI will write the script, generate voiceover, choose stock footage, add animated captions, and produce the final MP4.

### 🔹 InVideo AI Prompt: Video 1 (Technical Demonstration)
```text
Create a 90-second technical demonstration video about a software engineering mobile app called "Farmora".

Topic: Farmora - Smart AgriTech Mobile App Technical Architecture & System Demo
Target Audience: University software engineering evaluation panel, examiners, and software architects.
Tone: Professional, educational, authoritative, and tech-focused.
Voiceover: Clear, articulate male voice with neutral global English accent.

Key Sections to Cover:
1. Introduction (0:00-0:15): Introduce Farmora, a multi-platform mobile application built using Flutter 3 and Firebase Serverless backend for Sri Lankan agricultural supply chains.
2. Architecture & State Management (0:15-0:35): Highlight Clean Architecture, reactive Flutter UI rendering at 60fps, Cloud Firestore real-time snapshot streams, and Cloudinary media cloud processing.
3. Farmer Harvest & QR Traceability (0:35-0:55): Show field harvest video capture in the app, video compression, and automated cryptographic QR batch sticker generation for farm-to-shelf traceability.
4. Wholesale Procurement & Smart Escrow (0:55-1:15): Demonstrate buyer inspecting video proof of crop freshness, depositing funds into secure escrow via Firestore rules, and real-time cold-chain IoT temperature tracking during transport.
5. Delivery & Instant Settlement (1:15-1:30): Show digital proof-of-delivery (photo + signature) and instant two-second escrow fund release to farmer's wallet upon delivery verification.

Visual Style: Modern tech startup, high-tech Flutter UI mockups on smartphone screens, cloud diagrams, clean serverless badges, and realistic agricultural logistics. Include dynamic captions and high-tech upbeat ambient music.
```

Visual Style: Cinematic documentary footage of lush green Asian farms, hardworking farmers, modern refrigerated logistics trucks, bustling supermarkets, and clean mobile app screens. Cinematic inspirational background score.

# 🎙️ 3. Ready-to-Use Voiceover Narration Scripts (For ElevenLabs / Murf.ai)

Copy and paste these scripts into **ElevenLabs** (`elevenlabs.io`) or any TTS tool using the **"Adam"** or **"Rachel"** voice:

### 🎙️ Script 1: Technical Demonstration (90 Seconds)
```text
Welcome to Farmora. Built on Flutter 3 and Firebase serverless architecture, Farmora creates an ultra-responsive, real-time digital bridge connecting agricultural producers, wholesale buyers, and logistics networks across Sri Lanka.

At the core of our front-end is Clean Architecture, ensuring complete separation of concerns and maintaining a solid 60 frames per second on both budget smartphones and modern tablets.

In the field, farmers record verifiable video proof of harvest freshness. Our mobile client compresses the video stream, uploads it directly to Cloudinary media cloud, and generates a tamper-evident cryptographic QR batch identity.

When a wholesale buyer places an order, funds are deposited into an automated escrow vault governed by granular Cloud Firestore security rules. Payment is strictly locked.

During transit, the Transporter Fleet Hub streams IoT telematics and reefer temperatures in real-time, instantly flagging any thermal breaches.

Finally, upon QR scanning and photographic Proof-of-Delivery, escrow funds are released to the farmer in under two seconds. Farmora is built for performance, security, and true multi-platform scalability.
```

### 🎙️ Script 2: Business & Investor Pitch (90 Seconds)
```text
In Sri Lanka, over 30% of harvested vegetables rot before ever reaching a store shelf. At the same time, hardworking smallholder farmers lose up to 40% of their earnings to predatory broker cartels at physical economic centres. Food security is at risk.

Meet Farmora — the digital operating system for modern agricultural commerce.

Farmora bypasses opaque middlemen by directly connecting rural farmers with supermarket chains, hotels, and wholesale buyers. Through video-verified harvest listings, buyers get certified freshness, while farmers receive guaranteed market pricing.

Our integrated cold-chain fleet hub monitors refrigerated trucks in real-time, eliminating transit spoilage. And with our built-in escrow engine, farmers never face delayed payments or bad debts again.

Our business model is scalable and diversified: we generate revenue through a 2.5% marketplace commission, a 3.5% logistics matching fee, and enterprise supply chain SaaS. With over 1.5 million farmers across Sri Lanka, Farmora targets a 420-million-dollar digital trade pipeline.

Fair income for farmers. Guaranteed quality for buyers. Zero wasted harvest.

Farmora — connecting soil to shelf.
```
