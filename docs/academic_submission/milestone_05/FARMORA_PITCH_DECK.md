### Slide 2: The Agricultural Crisis in Sri Lanka
- **The Problem:**
  - **40% Loss to Middlemen:** Fragmented broker cartels at physical economic centres exploit smallholder farmers.
  - **30% Post-Harvest Spoilage:** Lack of verified cold-chain logistics causes fresh perishables to rot during transit.
  - **Zero Traceability:** Supermarkets and hospitality buyers cannot verify chemical safety, harvest date, or origin.
  - **Payment Defaults:** High risk of bad debts and delayed cash disbursements.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — Farmora Ecosystem
- **Direct Digital Marketplace:** Farmers sell directly to supermarkets, hotel syndicates, and bulk buyers.
- **Field Video Verification:** Farmers upload 10-second proof-of-freshness video directly from the field.
- **Cryptographic Batch QR:** Unique QR codes attached to crates verify harvest date, farmer identity, and agronomy data.
- **Guaranteed Escrow Protection:** Funds locked at order placement; released within 2 seconds of verified delivery.
- **Integrated Cold-Chain Fleet Hub:** Real-time IoT temperature monitoring across all transport reefers.

---

### Slide 4: Multi-Role Experience
- **For Farmers:** 1-tap listing, voice search, market price ticker, instant escrow payouts.
- **For Wholesale Buyers:** Transparent grades, field video inspection, bulk procurement, dispute resolution.
- **For Fleet Transporters:** Route matching, vehicle & driver depot management, live GPS, digital POD.
- **For Admin / Regulators:** Escrow treasury settlement, user KYC validation, dispute resolution tribunal.

---

### Slide 5: Technical Excellence & Architecture
- **Front-End:** Flutter 3.x (Multi-platform compilation to Android Release APK & Progressive Web App).
- **Back-End:** Firebase Serverless (Cloud Firestore real-time streams, Cloud Storage, Authentication).
- **Media Engine:** Cloudinary API for optimized video streaming and automatic cleanup upon order fulfillment.
- **Security:** Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced at Firestore engine level.
- **Offline First:** Local caching enables uninterrupted harvest logging in low-connectivity rural farms.
