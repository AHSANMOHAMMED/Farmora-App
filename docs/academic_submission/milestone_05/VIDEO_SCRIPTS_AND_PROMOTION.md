## 3. Video 1: Technical Demonstration (Duration: 90 Seconds)
**Intended Audience:** Technical Evaluators, Software Architects, Examination Panel  
**Tone:** Informative, Professional, Architecture-Focused  

- **[0:00 - 0:15] Architecture Overview:**
  - *Visual:* Clean high-level architecture diagram illustrating the Flutter client connecting to Firebase Cloud Functions, Firestore real-time listeners, and Cloudinary media processing.
  - *Voiceover:* "Welcome to Farmora. Built on Flutter and Firebase serverless architecture, Farmora creates an ultra-responsive, real-time bridge connecting producers, wholesale buyers, and logistics networks across Sri Lanka."
- **[0:15 - 0:40] Farmer Flow & Barcode Generation:**
  - *Visual:* Screen recording of the Farmer adding a harvest. Showing the live camera capturing fresh produce video, uploading to Cloudinary, and instantly generating an encrypted batch QR code.
  - *Voiceover:* "Farmers record video proof of freshness right in the field. Our system generates a cryptographic QR batch identity that tracks crop grade, harvest timestamp, and soil parameters."
- **[0:40 - 1:10] Buyer Escrow & Cold-Chain Telematics:**
  - *Visual:* Buyer browsing the catalog, streaming the harvest video preview, and depositing funds into escrow. Screen splits to the Transporter Fleet Hub with live Google Maps tracking and real-time cold-storage temperature graphs.
  - *Voiceover:* "Buyers inspect harvest video directly before purchase. Escrow locks payment until delivery. In the Fleet Hub, IoT telemetry streams reefer temperatures in real-time, preventing transit spoilage."
- **[1:10 - 1:30] Proof-of-Delivery & Escrow Release:**
  - *Visual:* Transporter captures recipient signature and delivery photo. The order status updates instantly via Firestore snapshot, and escrow funds are immediately transferred to the farmer's balance.
  - *Voiceover:* "Upon QR scan and photographic Proof-of-Delivery, escrow funds release automatically in under two seconds. Farmora is built for performance, security, and true multi-platform scalability."

## 4. Video 2: Business & Investor Pitch (Duration: 90 Seconds)
**Intended Audience:** Venture Investors, Agricultural Policy Makers, Business Evaluators  
**Tone:** Inspiring, Urgent, High Energy, Commercial Focus  

- **[0:00 - 0:20] The Problem:**
  - *Visual:* Documentary footage of Sri Lankan vegetable markets, overflowing crates, and disappointed farmers negotiating with brokers.
  - *Voiceover:* "In Sri Lanka, over 30% of harvested vegetables rot before reaching the shelf, while hardworking farmers lose up to 40% of their income to predatory intermediaries. Food security is at risk."
- **[0:20 - 0:45] The Solution:**
  - *Visual:* Fast-paced cinematic showcase of Farmora on mobile devices: vibrant green UI, 1-tap ordering, transparent price comparison.
  - *Voiceover:* "Meet Farmora — the digital operating system for agricultural commerce. We bypass broker cartels, directly connecting smallholders with supermarkets, hotels, and bulk buyers through video-verified trading."
- **[0:45 - 1:15] Business Model & Traction:**
  - *Visual:* Infographics displaying the 2.5% marketplace commission, 3.5% logistics dispatch fee, and expanding TAM across South Asia.
  - *Voiceover:* "Our multi-sided business model captures a 2.5% transaction commission, freight matching fees, and enterprise supply chain SaaS. With over 1.5 million farmers across Sri Lanka, Farmora targets a $420 million digital trade pipeline."
- **[1:15 - 1:30] Call to Action:**
  - *Visual:* Farmer smiling in a lush green field holding a smartphone, with the Farmora logo and download link.
  - *Voiceover:* "Fair pricing for farmers. Guaranteed quality for buyers. Zero wasted food. Join us in transforming agriculture. Farmora — connecting soil to shelf."
